• Court Case Monitoring System

  • Department sync case information and make updates, set alerts.
  • Integrated with the High court Information System to receive updated status and final orders.
  • The Court Case Monitoring System(CCMS) software is the digital diary, Which keeps everything in organised and easily accessible.
